AquaCatch Methodology & Engineering

1. Multi-Criteria Evaluation (MCE) for Pond Siting

Pond candidates are scored based on a combination of high upstream flow accumulation (60%), gentle slope ≤ 8° for stable earthen excavation (30%), and valley floor elevation (10%). Spatial Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) ensures candidates are spaced at least 100 meters apart.

2. D8 Inverted Upstream Watershed Delineation

From the candidate outlet, our reverse-flow CSR graph algorithm traces all upstream grid cells whose natural runoff drains into the proposed pond location, generating an exact high-resolution watershed boundary polygon.

3. Expected Water Volume (Rational Runoff Method)

Expected annual collectible water volume is computed as:

V = C × (P / 1000) × A
  • V: Collectible water runoff volume in cubic meters (m³) and Megaliters (ML).
  • C: Runoff coefficient representing soil permeability (0.15 for sand, 0.35 for clay-loam).
  • P: Annual rainfall depth in millimeters (mm).
  • A: Delineated catchment area in square meters (m²).
